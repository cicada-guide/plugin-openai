# Alabama HB591 — 2026 Regular Session

HB591 concerns supplemental Rural Health Transformation Program appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026. Cicada Guide records its status as **Passed**, dated **April 13, 2026**. The latest document is labeled **Enrolled**, dated April 9. Its certification records House passage April 1 and Senate passage April 8. The retrieved records do not establish gubernatorial approval or an act number; the enrolled label alone does not establish enactment.

The brief below uses only configured guide-public MCP results retrieved November 2, 2026.

**Text and funding.** The complete enrolled text was returned and read (7,298 characters; no remaining text chunks). It appropriates up to **$203,404,327 in federal funds**, identified in the text as coming from the One Big Beautiful Bill Act of 2025 (P.L. 119-21), to the Alabama Department of Economic and Community Affairs (ADECA). Sections 2–4 carry forward unspent funds and reappropriate amounts remaining September 30, 2026, for the same purposes in the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2026. To adjust initiative allocations, ADECA must obtain written concurrence from a majority of three officials—the Finance Director and the chairs of the House and Senate General Fund budget committees—then seek CMS approval. ADECA must comply with CMS requirements if approval is modified or withheld and with applicable federal funding conditions.

Section 5 creates a joint legislative oversight committee, co-chaired by the two General Fund budget committee chairs, with committee, leadership, minority, and appointed representation. It must organize within 30 days after the act takes effect. Its co-chairs may obtain implementation reports and data and conduct hearings. Section 6 specifies immediate effectiveness upon passage and gubernatorial approval, or otherwise becoming law. This is the text's effective-date condition, not confirmation that it occurred. Source: [enrolled text, sections 1–6](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2026RS/HB591-enr.pdf).
