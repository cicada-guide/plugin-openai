Alabama HB591 — 2026 Regular Session

HB591 concerns supplemental Rural Health Transformation Program appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026. Cicada Guide records its status as **Passed**, dated **April 13, 2026**. The latest document is labeled **Enrolled**, dated April 9, 2026. Its certification states that the House passed it April 1 and the Senate passed it April 8. These records do not establish gubernatorial approval or enactment. [Enrolled text](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2026RS/HB591-enr.pdf)

The complete enrolled text was readable through guide-public. Section 1 appropriates **up to $203,404,327 in federal funds** from the One Big Beautiful Bill Act of 2025 (P.L. 119-21) to the Alabama Department of Economic and Community Affairs (ADECA), allocated as follows:

Sections 2–4 carry unspent funds forward and reappropriate funds remaining on September 30, 2026, for the same purposes in the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2026. Reallocations require written concurrence from a majority of three officials—the Finance Director and the chairs of the House and Senate general-fund budget committees—followed by a request for Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services approval. ADECA must follow CMS requirements if approval is modified or withheld, and spending remains subject to federal requirements. [Enrolled text, sections 1–4](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2026RS/HB591-enr.pdf)

Section 5 creates a Joint Legislative Oversight Committee on Rural Health Transformation Program Funds, co-chaired by those two budget committee chairs, with legislative leadership, committee, and minority representation. Its co-chairs may obtain implementation reports and data and conduct hearings. The organizational meeting is required within 30 days after the act’s effective date. Section 6 provides for immediate effectiveness upon passage and gubernatorial approval, or otherwise becoming law; it does not establish that either occurred. [Enrolled text, sections 5–6](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2026RS/HB591-enr.pdf)
