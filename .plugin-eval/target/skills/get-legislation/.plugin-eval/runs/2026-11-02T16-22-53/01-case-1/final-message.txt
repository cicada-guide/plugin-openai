Found **Alabama HB591, 2026 Regular Session**: “Rural Health Transformation Program supplemental appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026.”

The bill would appropriate up to **$203,404,327** in federal rural health funding to ADECA and create a legislative oversight committee.

**Recorded status:** Passed, dated April 13, 2026. The returned evidence does not establish governor approval or enactment.
