# Cicada Guide for ChatGPT — landing draft

Unpublished draft; publisher wording requires owner confirmation before hosting.

Cicada Guide helps you research U.S. state bills, read available text, review recorded votes, and find recorded legislator contacts in ChatGPT. Intended plugin publisher: Forrest Satterfield. Adapted components retain Cicada Bot, LLC copyright notices.

Name a state, session and bill number, or a legislator and state. Explore bill results, bill workspaces, legislator records and contact cards. Answers disclose unavailable data and link returned sources. Records can lag official activity.

The plugin is read-only, connects anonymously, and involves no payments or purchases. It does not cover Congress, identify representatives by address, rank legislators, predict votes, or contact officials.
