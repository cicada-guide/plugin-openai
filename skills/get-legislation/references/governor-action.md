# After passage: general rules and individual outcomes

Use get_bill_dossier's returned governor_action for a state's general deadlines, unsigned-bill defaults, and effective-date guidance. If null, say that the rule is not recorded. Do not supply a rule from memory or a static table.

Present summary and note as general rules only. Never compute a bill's deadline without returned presentment and adjournment evidence, or infer that a governor signed, vetoed, or allowed a bill to become law from elapsed time. An enrolled document is not enactment evidence. Report dated bill status and history as recorded and disclose conflicts or missing confirmation. A bill's returned text may specify its effective date; do not substitute a general rule for a provision actually read.
